\subsection*{Abstract}

\note{TO DO: draft written from the thesis content, revise the wording and the
claims of the last paragraph once the calibrated comparison is finished.}

Comparing clinical trial designs is not straightforward when they differ in sample size,
power and type one error at the same time, and it becomes harder still when the design is
adaptive and the sample size is itself random. This thesis studies Bayesian two arm designs
with a Normal endpoint that borrow historical information on the control arm, and evaluates
them on a common per patient scale.

Borrowing is carried out with meta analytic predictive priors and their robust mixture
extensions. Designs are assessed through average operating characteristics, which separate
the analysis prior used to form the posterior from the design prior used to describe the
plausible true states of nature. Closed form expressions for the average type one error and
the average power are derived under Normal priors, together with three properties of the
averaged metrics. Average metrics lie closer to one half than their conditional
counterparts, the average type one error is controlled exactly at the nominal level when the
design and analysis priors coincide, and it degenerates to one half when the design prior
becomes vague while borrowing remains active.

Designs are then compared through the experimental unit information index, which normalises
the diagnostic odds ratio by the number of patients a design requires and therefore places
designs of different size on the same footing. The framework is developed for fixed designs
under both a single criterion and a dual criterion, and is then extended to group sequential
designs with one and with two interim analyses. Thresholds and sample sizes are calibrated
by simulation, using common random numbers so that the average type one error becomes a
smooth monotone function of the threshold and can be inverted by root finding, with the
threshold recalibrated at every candidate sample size.

The methods are illustrated throughout on a running example in Crohn's disease.
